Editorial policy

lifelongpep publishes non-clinical educational content to help people ask better questions about GLP-1 readiness and metabolic health.

Last reviewed: 13 October 2026. Content is general education, not medical advice or a substitute for an appropriately qualified clinician.

Scope and status

We label the product's current status accurately. The public beta offers general guides, readiness checklists, discussion prompts, and optional email updates. It does not provide consultations, prescriptions, clinical monitoring, fulfilment, or personalised treatment recommendations.

How we make claims

We aim for clear, proportionate language. We avoid guaranteed outcomes, miracle claims, and wording that makes medicines, peptides, supplements, recovery tools, or wearables sound risk-free. When evidence is uncertain, limited, evolving, or context-dependent, we say so.

Sources and review

When a page makes a factual medical or regulatory claim, we aim to link to an appropriate primary or authoritative source where practical. We review pages when their product status, source evidence, safety context, or material claims change. No clinician has reviewed or endorsed a page unless that page explicitly says so.

Editorial independence

Educational content should not be written to imply that a person needs a specific medicine, intervention, provider, or product. Commercial or partnership material, if introduced, should be clearly distinguished from editorial material.

Corrections

Email hello@lifelongpep.fit with a correction, source, or concern. We review substantive reports and update or clarify material when warranted.
