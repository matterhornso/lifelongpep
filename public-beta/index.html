Public-interest beta

Clarity before care.

The lifelongpep public beta is a learning and launch-updates programme for people in India who want better questions about GLP-1 readiness and metabolic health.

This is not clinical care. We do not collect medical histories, symptoms, lab reports, medication details, or payment information through this beta. We do not provide medical advice, diagnoses, prescriptions, bookings, or fulfillment.

What you can use today

  • Plain-language educational issues and guides.
  • GLP-1 readiness checklists and questions to take to a qualified doctor.
  • Optional email updates about the public beta and future launch milestones.
  • A way to share feedback, corrections, or partnership interest.

What is not available

  • Doctor consultations or a doctor-patient relationship.
  • Personalised treatment, medicine recommendations, or dose changes.
  • Eligibility decisions, pharmacy access, payment, or delivery.
  • Symptom monitoring or urgent clinical support.

How we use your email

When you opt in, we store your email and the interest category you select to send relevant public-beta and launch updates. Read the privacy notice, or email hello@lifelongpep.fit to ask a question, request removal, or report a concern.
