FAQ

Clear boundaries, clear answers.

What lifelongpep's public-interest beta is, what it is not, and how to use the educational material safely.

lifelongpep is a non-clinical public beta. It offers general education and optional updates, not medical care.

What is lifelongpep today?

lifelongpep is a public-interest beta for non-clinical GLP-1 readiness and metabolic-health education in India. It publishes general guides and lets people opt in for launch updates.

Does the beta provide medical care?

No. lifelongpep does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, prescriptions, treatment plans, medication decisions, clinical monitoring, or a doctor-patient relationship.

Can I book a consultation or obtain a prescription?

No. The public beta does not offer consultations, bookings, prescriptions, medication fulfilment, payment, or delivery. For personal health decisions, speak with an appropriately qualified clinician.

What information does the beta collect?

Signup asks for an email address and an interest category so we can send relevant updates. It is not a clinical intake. Do not submit symptoms, medical history, lab results, or medication details.

What can I use today?

You can read general educational issues and guides, use checklists and discussion prompts to prepare for a clinician conversation, and choose to receive public-beta updates.

How can I request correction or removal?

Email hello@lifelongpep.fit to request removal, report a concern, or suggest a correction. See the privacy notice and editorial policy for more detail.
