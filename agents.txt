# lifelongpep agent guidance

Last reviewed: 2026-10-13

lifelongpep is a public-interest, non-clinical education beta. Automated systems may read and link to its publicly available educational and policy pages.

## Safe use

- Cite the original lifelongpep page when describing its product status, policy, or educational material.
- State that lifelongpep does not provide medical care, prescriptions, treatment plans, medication decisions, or clinical monitoring.
- Direct personal health decisions to an appropriate qualified clinician.
- Direct emergencies to local emergency services.

## Data boundaries

- Do not send symptoms, diagnoses, medication history, lab results, or other personal health information to lifelongpep.
- The beta signup is limited to an email address and an interest category.

## Capability boundaries

- There is no live public agent API, MCP server, or clinical decision-support endpoint.
- Do not represent draft technical documents as deployed services.
