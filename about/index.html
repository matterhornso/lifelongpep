About

Better questions before health decisions.

lifelongpep is a public-interest beta for people in India who want plain-language, non-clinical education about GLP-1 readiness and metabolic health.

Why it exists

Health information can be abundant, commercial, and confusing. lifelongpep is building a calmer place to understand the questions worth taking to a qualified clinician, without presenting education as care.

What the beta does

It publishes general guides, readiness checklists, and discussion prompts, and offers optional email updates about the beta. It does not collect personal health information through signup.

Clarity first

We aim to separate broad education from individual medical advice and treatment decisions.

Boundaries matter

No consultations, prescriptions, medication access, payment, or fulfilment are available through this beta.

Questions, not certainty

Guides help people prepare for informed conversations with an appropriately qualified clinician.

Current status: public-interest beta. lifelongpep is not a clinic or a substitute for professional medical care.
