The active publishing plan is [the 30-day LinkedIn and Instagram calendar](social-calendar-30-days.md). It replaces the earlier consult-access calendar because lifelongpep is now a non-clinical public beta.

## Editorial pillars

1. Better questions before a medical decision.
2. Muscle preservation, strength, food, and recovery as part of the conversation.
3. Follow-up, maintenance, and stopping questions.
4. Clear public-beta boundaries: education and updates, not care or health-data collection.
5. Founder learning and requests for corrections to public education.
