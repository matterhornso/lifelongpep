# Social media guardrails

## What we publish

- General education on GLP-1 readiness, metabolic health, muscle preservation, recovery, and questions to discuss with a qualified doctor.
- Clear statements that lifelongpep is a non-clinical public beta.
- Founder learning, editorial corrections, and requests for feedback on educational material.
- Links to the public beta, privacy notice, FAQ, and relevant education pages.

## What we do not publish

- Medicine names, dosages, prices, availability, delivery, or access claims.
- Before-and-after imagery, weight-loss promises, or testimonials about outcomes.
- Personal medical advice, diagnosis, or responses to medical questions in comments or direct messages.
- Calls to send health history, laboratory reports, medication lists, symptoms, or photographs.
- Influencer endorsements or paid promotions that could be read as promotion of a prescription medicine.

## Comment and DM response

Use this response when someone asks for personal medical guidance:

> Thanks for asking. lifelongpep cannot give personal medical advice or assess whether a treatment is right for you. A qualified doctor can help with that. Our public-beta resources offer general questions you may want to discuss: https://lifelongpep.fit/glp-1-readiness-checklist/

Use this response when someone sends medical information:

> Please do not send personal health information here. lifelongpep does not collect it through social media or the public beta. Please speak with a qualified doctor for personal guidance.

## Review before publishing

1. Check that the post has one educational idea and one safe link.
2. Remove claims of access, results, safety for an individual, price, or clinical suitability.
3. Add the public-beta boundary when the post refers to lifelongpep.
4. Use the social UTM format in the 30-day calendar.
5. Keep a screenshot, caption, link, date, and resulting metrics in the publishing log.
