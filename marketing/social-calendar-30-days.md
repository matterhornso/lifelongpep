## Weekly operating rhythm

- Monday: publish LinkedIn between 8:00 and 10:00 IST. Reply to relevant comments with the saved safe-response template.
- Tuesday or Wednesday: publish Instagram between 12:00 and 14:00 IST. Save the cover, caption, and final slides in the publishing log.
- Friday: publish the second LinkedIn post. Spend 20 minutes engaging with clinicians, evidence-focused writers, and relevant public-health conversations without promoting medicine access.
- Friday afternoon: record visits, beta signups, issues subscriptions, saves, shares, comments, and profile visits. Use the UTM content values to compare posts.

## Captions and visual rules

- Use a single claim per slide and keep the visual sparse.
- Include "General education. Not personal medical advice." on the final slide of every carousel and reel.
- Do not use body-comparison images, injections, medicine packaging, price graphics, or screenshots of private messages.
- Use founder photography, typographic cards, simple checklist layouts, and source-linked editorial corrections instead.
