Privacy notice

This notice describes how lifelongpep handles the small amount of information collected through its public-interest beta. Last updated: 31 July 2026.

Do not submit medical history, symptoms, lab reports, prescription details, or other sensitive health information through the public-beta forms or email.

What we collect

If you opt in, we collect your email address and the interest category you select. Our forms may also receive the page you used to sign up and campaign parameters that help us understand how people found the beta.

Why we use it

We use this information to send the public-beta and launch updates you asked for, understand aggregate interest, operate the waitlist, and respond to enquiries or removal requests.

How to stop or ask a question

Email hello@lifelongpep.fit with the email address you used and your request to access, correct, or remove your beta information, or to stop updates. We will handle requests through the contact channel while the beta is operating.

Service providers and security

We use service providers to host the website, measure aggregate site use, and process form submissions. Access is limited to people who need it to operate the beta. We do not sell public-beta contact information.

Changes and concerns

We may update this notice as the beta changes. Material changes will be reflected by the date above. For a privacy or public-beta concern, email hello@lifelongpep.fit.
