Medical disclaimer

lifelongpep is a non-clinical public-interest beta. Its pages are general education, not medical care or individual medical advice.

Do not use lifelongpep content to start, stop, dose, switch, or source a medicine, supplement, peptide, or other health intervention.

No medical advice or clinical relationship

lifelongpep does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, monitor, or make individual eligibility decisions. Reading a guide, joining the beta, or emailing lifelongpep does not create a doctor-patient relationship.

Talk to an appropriate clinician

Questions about your symptoms, medical history, test results, medicines, pregnancy, treatment choices, or side effects should be discussed with an appropriately qualified clinician. Educational content cannot account for your individual circumstances.

Emergency care

If you think you are experiencing a medical emergency, seek help from local emergency services or a local emergency department immediately. Do not wait for a reply from this website.

Data boundary

The public-beta signup is not a clinical intake. Do not submit symptoms, medical records, lab results, medication details, or other personal health information through it.
